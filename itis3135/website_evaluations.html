Evaluations

New York Times Games

  • Contrast: Mostly black-on-white. Dark gray text used for supplementary information. Game icons have non-white backgrounds, but are light enough for black text to stand out.
  • Repetiton: Every game card follows the same general format, with an icon-title top half and user actions on the bottom half. Some games have more actions (e.g. an archive) that somewhat break the symmetry.
  • Alignment: Game cards are pleasantly arranged in a grid, whose bounds match with the featured article and archived games below it.
  • Proximity: Each game card has a notable but not-overbearing margin to the rest, which is equal both horizontally and vertically. Different sections (games to article, article to archive etc.) have wider margins.

Saint Vincent Ministry of Foreign Affairs

  • Contrast: Mostly black-on-white. Visa Entry Application button has a transparent background when hovered without changing the text color, making it very hard to read. At the bottom, the St. Vincent flag's blue blends into the black background without a border; also clicking on the flag leads to a broken link.
  • Repetiton: All three buttons on the top image (Missions, Visa, CSME) have compeletely different effects when hovered over. The Latest News articles behave differently depending on the amount of text; on some the "Read more" button is to the right of the image, on some it's below, and on some even the text starts wrapping below the image.
  • Alignment: The "Page x of 130" text is completely off-center compared to its corresponding page selector. The buttons on the right (below The Honourable Bramble) being stacked vertically, centered, and all different widths makes it hard to realize that they're grouped together.
  • Proximity: The top navbar has a weird amount of bottom padding. On the contrary, the two footers don't have nearly enough padding from each other, with the "EPA Implementation Unit" text being uncomfortably close to the bottom of its box.
